About Superday Brief

Superday Brief is a free daily market briefing for students recruiting for banking, sales & trading and buy-side roles. Each edition is put together from coverage across major financial outlets. Duplicate stories are merged, then ranked by how widely they're covered and how directly they affect rates, earnings, deals and the broad market.

Headlines and summaries are written by Superday Brief in our own words, with links to the original reporting. New editions publish before the market opens and after it closes on weekdays, plus a week-ahead edition on Sundays.

Headlines link to the original publishers. Summaries and talking points are written by Superday Brief and are for interview prep, not investment advice.
